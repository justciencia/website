## The mission

justciencia celebrates Latino scientists and engineers as the extraordinary people they are, honoring not only their discoveries but the long, often unseen paths that led them there. Each profile translates their research into plain language, using analogies rooted in Latino culture and everyday life, so the public can understand the science and see themselves in the people who do it. Free, bilingual, and created by a first-generation Mexican American PhD.

## How we write

**Plain language first.** We explain a term the first time it appears, choose a simple word when one will do, and never treat a reader as someone who "just doesn't get it." The explainers, graph guides, and paper summaries in [Stories]({{ '/stories/' | relative_url }}) follow the same approach.

**Analogies from everyday Latino life.** A good analogy lets you feel an idea before you learn its name. A few examples of how we think about it:

- A membrane that lets only certain ions through is like the doorman at a quinceañera who only lets in the guests on the list.
- A catalyst is like the comadre who knows the shortcut through the mercado. You still get from home to the stall, but with far less effort, and she isn't used up by the trip.
- An electric current is like water moving through the acequias that irrigate a field. The voltage is the push, and the current is how much water flows.

## Our process

Scientists review and approve their profiles in writing before anything is published.

1. We talk with the scientist, or they share their story in writing.
2. We draft the profile in plain language, including the path that led them here and the science itself.
3. The scientist reads the full draft and asks for any changes.
4. The scientist approves it in writing. An email is enough.
5. Only then do we publish it.

Know a scientist whose story should be told? You can [nominate a scientist]({{ '/scientists/#apply' | relative_url }}), including yourself. If you spot a mistake anywhere on the site, open an issue on this site's GitHub repository and we'll fix it.
